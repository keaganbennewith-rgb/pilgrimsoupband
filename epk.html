Short bio

Pilgrim Soup is a Durban-based rap-rock / alternative hip-hop band built around bass-driven grooves, live drums, guitars, and rap vocals. Formed in 2011 and active again in 2026, the band moves between original songs, crossover features, and festival-stage live energy.

For press, listings, and posters, use: Pilgrim Soup — Rap Rock / Alternative Hip-Hop from Durban, South Africa.
